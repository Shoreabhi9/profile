About Me

Hello! I'm Abhinav Mishra, a dedicated physics educator with a strong academic foundation in physics. With both B.Sc. (Honours) and M.Sc. degrees in Physics, I bring a deep understanding of the subject to my teaching.

I believe that physics is not just about formulas and equations—it's about understanding the universe around us. My teaching philosophy centers on making complex concepts simple, engaging, and relevant to everyday life.
